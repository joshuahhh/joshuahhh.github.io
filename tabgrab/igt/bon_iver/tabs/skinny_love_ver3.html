	I wanted to put this tab up because there are not really a lot of 
accurate tabs to this song on the internet.  This is close to how Justin 
Vernon (Bon Iver) plays it, but this is just the way I think it sounds best 
and is the easiest to play.  Don’t be discouraged if you aren’t able to 
play it right away-keep with it!  It’s a beautiful song and really fun to 
play.  If you’re more of a visual learner, go to YouTube and check our 
cougarman8’s instructional video.
	There’s no capo.  The tuning is pretty funky: CGEGCC.  If you want 
to play along with the recording, you will have to detune just a tad.
