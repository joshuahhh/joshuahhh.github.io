This one's easy to put together!

It's played on the record with all your strings tuned one half step low- not necessary 
but it sounds great down there. Point of interest, these are precisely the same chords 
used in Iron & Wine's hit "Upward Over the Mountain," and they're played in a similar 
manner.

Chords:
Em      x7900x
CM7     x3500x
G       35000x
Dsus4 6 x5700x

The rhythm is slow and simple, goes something like this, but some notes are barely 
there. Use your thumb and forefinger and strum the bass notes harder when you want to 
punctuate; always let them ring. Keep the treble very soft. And there's a lot of 
fingerboard noise on the track, so go ahead and slide and click and whatnot.
