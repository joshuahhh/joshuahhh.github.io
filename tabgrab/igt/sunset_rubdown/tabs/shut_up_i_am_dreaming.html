This is a tab I found on the Wolf Parade website, it is for the album 
version intro of Shut up I'm dreaming. It sounds great without a pick where 
you thumb the D string, and I use my index and middle to pluck the B and 
G's.  Sounds really amazing, I've seen the band play the song live and they 
never play it like the album, so it is tough to say if this is accurate to 
Spencer's original.
