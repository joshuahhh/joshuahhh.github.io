*TWO NOTES*

1. This tab is a representation of the recorded version of
Cannibal Resource. The live version features many variations
on the material presented here and can be figured out by using
what's written here as a springboard.

2. Because of the high notes and the capo on the 5th fret, the BRIDGE
is difficult (but not impossible) to play cleanly on a guitar that
doesn't allow easy access to the higher frets (i.e. up to 17).
