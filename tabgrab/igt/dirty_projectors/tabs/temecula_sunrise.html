LEGEND:

| ^  brush up
| s  slide
| h  hammer-on
| p  pull-off
| .  staccato
| R  let ring
| g  grace note (note preceding 'g', played faster than hammer-on)

Standard tuning
Capo on 7th fret

I've indicated rhythm loosely throughout this tab, but it's up to your ear to
figure out the tricky parts and tempo changes.  The intro feels like it's accented
every four eighths, so I've barred it off as if it was in 2/4.  The rest
of the song has a 6/8 swing to it, so everything but the intro has 2 beats
to a bar, 3 eights to each beat.  Have fun trying to sing to this!

Enjoy!
