Artist: Dirty Projectors
 Album: Bitte Orca
  Song: Two Doves

Tabbed by: Kris Ellestad

Site: knilstad.blogspot.com

This is a fairly simple song that requires basic fingerpicking
technique, i.e. thumb and forefinger, however independence of 
each finger on the right hand makes it more fluid.

I've nearly written out the entire song note-for-note, though
it's really the same material repeated with slight variations.

When in doubt, refer back to the recording!

| h  hammer-on
