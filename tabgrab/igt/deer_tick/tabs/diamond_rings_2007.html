Capo 4
For the strumming use a simple base-strum down-up style. Listen to the song for what 
sounds best.

To get it to sound more like the song, I do a hammer-on for the next note after the 
base note. So for G chord I hammer on 2nd fret on A string with 1st finger. For 
Dsus4/F# hammer on 2nd fret on G string with 2nd finger, etc. So you hammer on right 
after plucking the base note and before the strum. Don't do this for D and Em, just 
strum regular.
