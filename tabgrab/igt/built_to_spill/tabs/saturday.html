The trick to this song is that it is off-key.
I played the bass line on my guitar and tuned
the E string down until I was in key with the
song and then tuned my guitar to that.  This
tab is for the off-key tuning.  The guitar part
is not exact; you have to listen to the song to
get the rhythm.  There is another guitar playing
in the background.  Hopefully people will improve upon my laziness.
