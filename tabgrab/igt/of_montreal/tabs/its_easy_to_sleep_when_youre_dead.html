The wonderful last song on "The Bedside Drama". As with any Of
Montreal song, there are almost as many chords as there are words; 
I tried to be as detailed with the chords as I could, but I may 
have missed or misinterpreted a few. Listen along with his voice 
and try to add or subtract notes according to the melody, and 
change the chords to suit you.  (n/c) means stop playing and sing. 
I may easily be wrong about the capo, i didnt realize my guitar 
was a half step sharp when I transcribed this.  If anyone comes up 
with a regular version they should totally put it on the internet.
