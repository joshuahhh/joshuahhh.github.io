 Emaj         F#m/A
e--x--        e--x--

A--2--        A--4--
E--0--        E--5--

Note:
The first part of the song is just the Emaj, then as the song builds the two 
chords alternate.  The F#m can also be played without the A bass note, but I think 
it sounds a lot better with that in there, it yields a country thing.  (some tabs 
say it alternates between E and A, I respectfully disagree).
