Nothing intricate here, but the basic idea is just standard tuning, F#m - E.  This is 
the basic progression throughout the whole song.  Listen and you can pick up on the 
fills, etc.  The solo is pretty straightforward as well.  Some simple noodling on a 
pentatonic scale and you can achieve something similar to what JV had in mind.  As an 
exclusively acoustic guitar player, this is a fun song to play finger-style with some 
rolls.
