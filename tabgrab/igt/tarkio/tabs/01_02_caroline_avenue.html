This intro is hard to tab since it's so 'loose'. Basically, the single
notes below are ones you should emphasize between the strumming -
give it some practice and it will sound right.

e|--------------------------------

G|-2-2-2-0---x-------0----0-------
D|-0-0-----4-2-------0----0-------
A|-0-0-------0-----0h2--0h2-------
E|--------------------------------

The D throughout can often be replaced by a Dsus2 (x00230) or just D without
a 3rd (x0023x), and the A can be played as the Asus4 above (x02230) or (x02x30)
