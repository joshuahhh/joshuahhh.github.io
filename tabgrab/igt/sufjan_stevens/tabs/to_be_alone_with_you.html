Artist: Sufjan Stevens
 Album: Seven Swans
  Song: To Be Alone With You (ver 2)

Tabbed by: D. H. (dreq10@gmail.com)

Every tab I've ever seen of this song has used the same chords, and I always felt 
like they never sounded the same as when Sufjan plays it live or on his album. So, 
I sat down and made this tab by watching videos of him playing it live. I'm 95% 
sure that the chords are the same he uses. Other tabs have you playing chords on 
the higher strings, but I think playing it on lower strings really adds to the 
feeling of the song.

I've seen him play it with a capo on both the fourth and fifth frets, but on the 
album the capo is on the third. So just put it wherever you like the sound I 
guess. I prefer it on the fourth fret.

Sufjan plays the song by alternating down and up-strokes with his thumb, and the 
pad and fingernail of his index finger. Check out a live video to get an idea. It 
really helps reproduce his sound.

He also adds lots of little intricacies to the chords as he plays them. I think he 
improvises them, 'cause they're different almost every time. Because of that, I 
didn't try to tab them. As you play the song more you can develop your own version.

Lastly, there's a second guitar that comes in for the rest of the song after the 
first chorus that I didn't tab. It does some solo picking on the same chords that 
Sufjan is strumming.

Standard Tuning
All notes relative to capo.
