bar the chords, you'll see why:
   1st: just play chords to the main rhythm (same as the verse) 
	when you have this part right,
   2nd: then practice muting the E string leaning against it slightly 
with a
        finger while in chord position this adds the "chic" sound heard 
all
        the way through, marked with an X above the chords, then
   3rd: add the rest of the "chic-a" sound in between the 
lifting 
	up slightly on the strings during the appropriate up or down 
        stroke, giving the rhythm a triplet feel, essential to this 
section
