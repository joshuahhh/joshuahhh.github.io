Just got back from seeing him play this live, and (no offense) I feel like it's a lot more accurate 
than the tab below.
The basic chord pattern is:
G  A7  C  G
When you're on the C chord, use your pinky and play around with the B string.
The lick is pretty much
B--3-1-0-1-3-1-0
I've heard this song played at high pitches, so feel free to throw a capo on there. 
I don't have the Sweden album here to check it against, but I'm pretty sure that it 
goes like this through the whole song. Some live versions have a lot more improvisation 
on the outro, but you can do that stuff at your own will.
