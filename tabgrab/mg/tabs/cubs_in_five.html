Recently John stated on the forums that Cubs In Five was played in D, he just didn't have a guitar tuner at the time.
So, this is how I believe the song to be played, if you were to do it in D.
The timing is a little hard to catch on to at first, I suggest you listen to 
the song to get the rhythm. After that it's pretty simple, the verses and
choruses all follow the same riffs through to the end of the song.
