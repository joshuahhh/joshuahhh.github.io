------BLUEJAYS AND CARDINALS VERSION 3------

when this site was down for a few days, I really wanted to learn this song, so I set down to learn it myself. 
I came up with something, and me and my friend both agree that it sounds just like the album version.

instrumental demonstration of this tab: http://www.box.net/shared/l0lc3nlkyz
I play the intro + verse a couple of times, and at 00:51 I switch to the chorus.

tuning: CGCCGC. the two middle Cs are the same tone. 
capo on 1st fret
additionally, the guitar on the album version is a bit sharp.

NOTE: for every chord, I provide notes for all six strings, but as he strums very loosely, 
it is possible that he sometimes only plays the higher three or four strings and not all six 
(especially during the riffs.) listen to the record or figure out how you like to do it.
