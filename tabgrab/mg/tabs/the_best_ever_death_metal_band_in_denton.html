Tuning: Drop D (DADGBe)

From that last D, you can either go straight into the Main Riff, or use another normal D G A first (with a bit more intensity) and 
then go into the Main Riff.  John prefers the latter, but I like to go straight into the riff and then repeat it a second time.

Thanks to Jeff Kelley

On the recording, I believe John plays this with the top E string tuned a major second lower (to D) to get that droney open sound.

The chords are as follows:
   D(no 3rd)  G  A  (sometimes played A sus4)
d--0----------0-----(0)----------------------
B--3----------3--2--(3)----------------------
G--2----------0--2--(2)----------------------
D--0----------0--2--(2)----------------------
A-------------2--0--(0)----------------------
E-------------3------------------------------

The song is just those three chords, with a few fills that should be easy to figure out 
(they're all played on the B string with the high D playing an open drone)
