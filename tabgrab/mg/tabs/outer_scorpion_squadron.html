This is one of the more aurally complex Mountain Goats songs.
I'm pretty sure about the verse and chorus chords, but when the strings swell 
during the bridge it's a little hard to tell what's going on underneath, so those 
chords are basically just a guess. Also, I recommend playing the F/G chord 
with your thumb on the third fret of the E string, like so:
