Then The Letting Go

Tuning: Drop the high E down to D and capo at 2

Chords relative to capo:
D    : xx0230
Asus4: x02220
G    : 320000

The whole song is: D Asus4 G Asus4
Except between the two verses there is an extra Asus4 G

You could also play this without a capo, leaving the E open throughout, but I think it sounds better with the capo.
