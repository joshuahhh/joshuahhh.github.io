Updated:  the below is mostly correct, but I found that two changes will more closely match how John plays this song.

First, tune everything down a whole step, so that normal E = D
Second, change the "D" chord (actually B- (or with new tuning C#-) minor sus4 add6, I think) as follows:

e--0
B--7
G--7
D--9
A--0
E--x
