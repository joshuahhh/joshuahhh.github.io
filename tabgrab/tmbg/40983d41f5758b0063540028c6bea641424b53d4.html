This is a complicated song and I wanted to see if it could be arranged for guitar.  I hope
you enjoy it.  I've left the previous chord sheets at the bottom, since this arrangement might
be not what you're looking for.

This is roughly what is going on in the horn parts; the same riff is played more softly
during the verses.  I've arranged it to be playable on guitar, and though the fingerings
work out and all, it is still somewhat unnatural and takes practice.  The "040" in the 2nd
bar should be played as "0h4p0"; I just didn't want to expand the rhythm to 16th notes.
