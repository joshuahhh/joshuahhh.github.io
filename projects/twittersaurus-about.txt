Twittersaurus checks to see whether certain Twitter usernames are
available. Red means "taken", green means "free". It has three modes:

Thesaurus  -- Look up synonyms of a given word. (The thesaurus I'm
              currently using isn't too good. I should replace it.
              Meanwhile, in honor of our T-rex mascot, try "sexy".)

Numbers    -- Look up numbers. (Stops at 6000.)

Dictionary -- Look up words out of a dictionary. (98569 of them.)

This project is kind of sketchily coded. I hope you enjoy it.
