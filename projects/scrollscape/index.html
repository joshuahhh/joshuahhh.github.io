Scrollscape

Scrollable two-dimensional documents

Lost in the endless scroll of web documents and longing for embodied media where you can See the Whole, I built Scrollscape.

Try the demo.

I made Scrollscape for an assignment in CSE 599D. The paper featured in this demo is an early version of the Engraft paper presented at UIST 2023. Living Papers was used to produce the HTML content embedded in the demo; a short introduction to Scrollscape was included in Living Papers paper.
