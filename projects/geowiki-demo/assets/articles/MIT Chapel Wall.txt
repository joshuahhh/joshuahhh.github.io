Who would have thought there would be a free outdoor climbing wall, right at the heart of MIT's campus? Certainly not MIT... Although it may look like Eero Saarinen designed the brickwork on this curtain wall specifically for the benefit of climbers interested in practicing their traverses, buildering is apparently not an officially sanctioned activity on MIT property.

Feel free to give it a try, though. Who could blame you for climbing around on some sunny day, a perfect parallel to the Kresge Oval frisbee players? Or take a crash pad onto the alley side, where there's enough height for an engrossing game of Add-On.

One way or another, this wall is certainly a novelty...
