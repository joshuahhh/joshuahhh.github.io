Not quite a park, per se, but if you want someplace tranquil, lovely, and well-manicured, this place will do. Killian Court is the centerpiece of the <a href="event:MIT">MIT</a> campus, and is surrounded on three sides by the gargantuan neoclassical Main Group. You will find students out here, reading, hurrying between classes, and playing frisbee, as long as the weather is nice enough (though frisbee is more of an all-season activity than you might expect...).
