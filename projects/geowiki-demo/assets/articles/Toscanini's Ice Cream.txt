The people who run Toscanini's still haven't gotten over the time someone at the New York Times said they had the "best ice cream in the world". I mean, they have the quote splashed across their facade in torso-sized letters, for crying out loud. And the ice cream is certainly on the premium side of the spectrum. Best in the world? With those sorts of claims you can't help but get backlash. Still, try the Bourbon Vienna Finger if you get a chance.
