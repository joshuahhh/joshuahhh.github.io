Caltrain Camera Array

Shooting a cell-phone video out of the window of Caltrain, I inadvertantly created a miles-long camera array (like Lytro, but bigger). Using the information contained in these video frames, I constructed new panoramic perspectives on the landscape passing by, slicing and rearranging rays of light into impossible projections.

This page features two demonstrations of this idea. I hope you enjoy playing with them.
