Completion rates & learner satisfaction

Josh Horowitz (September 2015)

How much does completion rate tell us about learner satisfaction?

Let's start with a drastically simplified model, since those make for fun toys. Suppose:

  1. Every learner ends up at some 'final satisfaction level'.
  2. This satisfaction level is distributed normally.
  3. If a learner's final satisfaction level is below a certain threshold (and only in this case), they will drop out of the course.
  4. If a learner's final satisfaction level is above a certain threshold (and only in this case), they will recommend it to friends and be a satisfied advocate.

Drag the 'satisfaction distribution' around and see what happens to the completion rate and the recommendation rate. This model determines a relationship between these two rates, shown on the right. What does this relationship look like? What does this curve say about the effectiveness of using completion rate as a proxy for learner satisfaction?
